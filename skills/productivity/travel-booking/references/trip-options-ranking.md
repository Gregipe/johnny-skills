# Trip Options & Ranking (Greg)

Method for when Greg asks for destination options for a family/group trip — not one destination's logistics.

## Inputs to capture first

Traveler count + ages, dates, origin city, premium level, what the trip must contain (chill vs activity split), flight-vs-drive preference. Voice notes arrive un-transcribed — transcribe before planning (see whatsapp-gateway-ops).

## Option generation

1. Research Greg's own anchors properly (he usually names 2-3: e.g. Goa, Vizag, Alibag).
2. Then a LATERAL pass: same DNA (short transfer, premium-enough for guests, chill + activity) but different texture — beach+casino+forts (Daman), lake/palace/shopping (Udaipur), vineyard country (Nashik), quiet beach (Gokarna), backwaters (Kumarakom). Always include one wildcard: the most different option that is still defensible on his brief.
3. Per option: what it is, top stay picks, 1-2 honest caveats. End the first reply with ONE blunt recommendation and a next step ("I can pull live rates for the two you shortlist").

## Ranking format Greg responds to

- Numbered list covering ALL options, each with 4-axis scores out of 10: Activity, Relaxation, Connectivity, Affordability, plus one-line justification.
- Apply an ACTIVITY GATE first for multi-day trips: can it genuinely fill 3-4 days with trips/sights/shopping/dining? Options that can't get flagged even when cheap ("it's a 2-day reset wearing a 4-day suit").
- End with a verdict separating best-on-brief vs value pick vs premium pick.
- When the ranking criteria evolve mid-conversation ("now also factor flight/drive costs"), re-rank from scratch rather than tweaking scores.

## Transport costing — the decider

- **Flights:** route fare guides give month bands — December is the highest month on most Indian leisure routes (Goa one-way ₹5-9k at Christmas, Kochi +40-80%, Udaipur December-average is the year's peak). Quote "typical price" bands from Google Flights / MakeMyTrip results; then multiply by the GROUP for the total, and add airport-to-resort cab legs.
- **Drives:** 7-8 pax needs two SUVs, ~₹4.5-5.5k/day each incl. driver, plus fuel/tolls ≈ ₹20/km. A 4-day round trip from Mumbai lands ≈ ₹45-60k total — usually ₹50k+ cheaper than flying 8 people.
- Present per-person AND total-family numbers; the comparison often flips the ranking.

## Peak-week warnings

Dec 20-31 is the busiest Indian travel stretch: premium resort inventory sells out, fares/hotels inflate 40-80%+. Name the booking deadline explicitly ("book this month, not next") and prefer options that dodge the peak (drives, non-beach-party destinations) when affordability matters.
