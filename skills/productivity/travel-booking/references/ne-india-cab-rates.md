# NE India Cab Market Rates & Vendors (Meghalaya/Assam)

## Benchmark sources

- TTAA (Tourist Transporter Association of Assam) rate chart — the industry's official ceiling:
ttaa.in/vehicle-rate-chart. Meghalaya tours, 5+ days: Sedan ₹4,600/day · Ertiga ₹5,300/day ·
Innova Crysta ₹6,300/day.
- Operator street bands (all-in per day, driver + fuel + tolls): Dzire/Sedan ₹3,000–4,300 ·
Ertiga ₹4,500–5,300 · Innova Crysta ₹5,500–6,500. meghalayacabs.com lists Ertiga ₹5,000/day all-in.
- Reference quotes received for 8 days / 7 nights all-in: Dzire 29–30K · Ertiga 38–40K ·
Innova 44–45K. Ertiga at 38K = ₹4,750/day — fair value, ~10% under the TTAA ceiling;
anything above the ceiling without justification is overpaying.

## Quote-evaluation checklist

- Driver night charge ≠ food: night charge covers lodging only; food is often ₹200–300/day extra.
- "All-inclusive" can assume AC off on hill climbs — confirm full-AC terms for hill routes.
- Get advance % and payment method in writing; first replies usually omit it.
- Evening pickup + morning drop bills as 8 days / 7 nights — standard practice, don't negotiate it.
- Send the SAME enquiry text to every vendor so quotes are comparable; replies land in Greg's
own WhatsApp, not the agent chat.
