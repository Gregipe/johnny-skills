# Meghalaya Destination Notes (family trips)

Region knowledge for planning or answering questions about Meghalaya trips. Greg has done the Shillong → Sohra → Shnongpdeng loop with family; recurring-destination depth.

## Two corridors — never mix bases

- **Sohra/Cherrapunji axis** (south of Shillong, ~1.5–2h drive): Mawkdok Valley, Arwah Cave, Nohkalikai, Mawsmai Cave, Seven Sisters, Kynrem Falls, Nongriat Double-Decker Root Bridge trek.
- **Dawki/Shnongpdeng corridor** (southeast, via Pynursla, 4–5h from Shillong): Shnongpdeng/Umngot boating, Mawlynnong village, Riwai Living Root Bridge, Phe Phe Falls, Krang Shuri (Jowai side).
- **Mawlynnong is on the Dawki corridor** — a 2.5–3h detour from a Sohra base. Slot it into the Shnongpdeng leg (en route or return leg), never on a Sohra day.
- Useful distances: Mawlynnong→Shnongpdeng ~1h; Phe Phe Falls ~25 min from Shnongpdeng; Krang Shuri ~1.5h detour each way from Shnongpdeng; Nongjrong viewpoint ~40 km from Shillong.

## Seasonality and daylight (November)

- Post-monsoon dry season: showers are rare and brief. Shillong 8–20°C; Mawphlang sits higher and runs a few degrees colder at morning start. Layers, not winter gear.
- **Daylight is the binding constraint, not weather**: dark by ~4:30–4:45 PM, sunrise ~5:25. Trek days need 7 AM starts; sunset viewpoints (Laitlum) must be hit before 4:30. When asked "will it rain / will it be cold", always answer with the daylight deadline too — it decides itinerary feasibility more than the forecast does.

## Guide rules

- **Mawphlang Sacred Grove: guide is MANDATORY** (village rule) — hired at the entrance counter, ~₹350 plus nominal per-head entry. No guide, no entry. Forest has no signposted paths; taboos apply (carry nothing out, not even a leaf); the guide is the interpretation layer (monoliths, ritual sites, off-limits areas).
- **David Scott Trail** (Mawphlang→Lad Mawphlang, 16 km, 4.5–6h): guide optional but recommended — confusing forest junctions, and the same village guides cover both grove and trail (hire once). Point-to-point, so the guide also coordinates the driver pickup at the far end. With kids, treat the full 16 km as aspiration — guides know the exits to shorten.
- Grove + full trail in one day = 7–8 hours on foot; only viable with an early start. With a child, recommend the partial trail.

## Family logistics

- **Gawooh Adventure (Shnongpdeng)**: riverside adventure stay — suspension bridge plus ~8 min walk from parking; guests asked to bring ONE small bag per person (caretaker can't haul suitcases); all bookings non-refundable. Cabin over tent in November: cold nights, attached bathroom. Camp rates are per-head; cabins are per-unit.
- **Call-only properties**: many Meghalaya stays publish no rates online (e.g. Sa-I-Mika Park) — pull the phone number from the site and recommend a call; verify with recent reviews (TripAdvisor/Agoda/Airbnb ratings) before endorsing.

## Itinerary gap checklist (vs typical Meghalaya loops)

Worth adding when missing:
- **Nongjrong sunrise** — sea-of-clouds viewpoint 40 km from Shillong; only clean slot is a pre-dawn on a departure morning (leave ~4:15, back by 7:30).
- **Phe Phe Falls** — ~25 min from Shnongpdeng; easy add after morning boating.
- **Kynrem Falls** — 12 km from Sohra near Seven Sisters; pairs with a recovery-day loop.
- **Elephant Falls** — inside Shillong, zero detour; fits any free hour.
- **Mawkdok zipline** — same stop as the valley viewpoint; kid-friendly.

Skip without guilt:
- **Krang Shuri** on a packed loop — spectacular turquoise pools but a 1.5h detour each way; only worth it with a full Dawki-base day.
- **Wah Kaba** — 275 steps for a modest falls; only if the route day is empty.
- **Wei Lynkshyar** — minor falls, skippable.
