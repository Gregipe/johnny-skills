## The two Indias

pfi = 25-35 salaried saver (2.5Cr flats, gold, FIRE, tax). IndiaFinance = 18-25 striver (loan apps, frozen accounts, family emergencies, bank friction). DEBT is the one theme that unites both — the single most consistent intensifying concern across every corpus.

## Debt problem statements (both subs, 2026, ~1/3 of ALL posts)

1. EMI > income — salaried over-leverage (42.9% of pfi debt posts)
2. Emergency borrower the system doesn't see (13.9%/16.6%) — 18-19yo, no CIBIL, no ITR → payday/app lenders
3. Loan-app/NBFC predation (5.1%/10.1%)
4. Education debt vs income reality (9.4%/7.2%, DOUBLED since 2024)
5. Family debt entanglement (parents' loans → children's problems, hidden debt)
6. Guarantor/co-signer traps
7. CIBIL damage & recovery (rising)
8. The exit tax — foreclosure/lien/penalty fees (doubled since 2024)

Cleanest statement: "The young Indian emergency borrower does not exist for the formal credit system — so they borrow from apps designed to trap them, from family who can't afford it, or from moneylenders nobody talks about."

Meta: the sub's emotional center moved from BUILDING wealth (rate my SIP, FIRE calculators) to DEFENDING it (emergency funds, gold, can-I-afford-X).
