## Reconciliation of all three validation runs (Sep-Oct YoY, H1 YoY, 2024 baseline)

- STRUCTURAL (every window agrees): salary/offer anxiety, degree-ROI anxiety (the two biggest, monotonic 2 yrs), AI fear (fastest riser, continuous climb), fresher hunt, ghosting, side-income, govt-job fallback, layoffs/PIP.
- 2025-SPIKE phenomena (earlier reads misattributed to 2026): age pressure (spiked 2025, retreating, still above 2024), toxic workplace (rose through 2025, plateaued ~13%), study abroad (2025 exit-mania deflated back to 2024 baseline — NOT a collapse of the abroad dream).
- NEWEST: the referral economy — the word "referral" quadrupled only in 2026; discourse caught up with practice.
- Tone shift: 2024 posts = exploration/aspiration ("what after engineering?"), 2026 = triage (layoffs, PIPs, referral begging). Same sub, different emotional register.

## Verdicts for content thesis

Rise with it: salary transparency rage, degree-ROI doubt, AI-proofing, fresher pipeline collapse, ghosting culture, side-income necessity, govt-job fallback. Peaked and settled high: age pressure, toxic workplace, abroad exodus. Rising silently: layoffs/PIP, mental health.
