## The 2-year arc (anxiety changed TYPE)

- 2024: "I over-borrowed" (bankruptcy posts, EMI>salary; blame self)
- 2025: "Life events hit my EMIs" (scams, co-applicant defaults, personal-loan mental toll)
- 2026: "The system may take my income while the EMI stays" — AI layoffs entered borrowing decisions explicitly ("Will home loan decline amid AI layoff threats", "personal loan for job relocation ft AI layoffs"). Fear moved from consequence-of-choices to systemic risk.

## Why amplifying (grounded Nov 2026)

1. Two jaws of the vise: income frozen (generalist/junior pay flat, new-grad hiring −65% vs 2019, AI roles +40-60% premium) while EMIs locked at high rates against rising metro prices (HPI +3.6% YoY, premium-led).
2. Layoffs became visible + AI-branded: ~125K tech jobs lost 2026 YTD, AI-cited cuts 12x in 2 yrs, Oracle ~12K India, TCS 12K.
3. Credit productization → invisible stacking: no-cost EMI/BNPL/loan apps ("quietly rented a lifestyle"; "EMI-SIP trap").
4. Family pressure underwrites over-borrowing: "parents want a 1.5Cr house before 35" (21yo earning 50K); property = proof of being settled.
5. Mental load became the explicit currency: threads titled "EMI destroying mental peace", "keeps you up at night", "really fucked up". EMI is now a sleep/marriage/family problem.

## One-line thesis statement
